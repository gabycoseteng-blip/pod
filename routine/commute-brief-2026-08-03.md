- EXELON'S PIPELINE DEFLATES — the queue reality-check story: "high-probability" DC load fell from 18GW (end-2025) to 11GW (Q2'26), -40% in two quarters; total future pipeline through 2027 fell from 43GW (May) to 25GW (Jul 30 earnings). (Utility Dive) Read: first real precedent for BTM renewable-colocated load under SB6 — curtailable, no DR pay, breaker-enforced — a template ERCOT will likely replicate across the 14GW CenterPoint queue.
