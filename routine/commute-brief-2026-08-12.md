## 6. DEEP DIVE — source: INBOX (Dave Friedman's Substack)
davefriedman-market-pricing-payment-certainty-not-gpus: Dave Friedman (independent AI-infrastructure finance analyst), "The Market Is Pricing Payment Certainty, Not GPUs" (Aug 10). Core argument: GPU-backed AI infrastructure debt isn't one uniform risk story — the market is bifurcating sharply by collateral structure. Senior, investment-grade-customer-backed project loans (CoreWeave's $8.5B DDTL 4.0, non-recourse, SOFR+225, A3/A(low)) trade like clean project finance, while non-investment-grade/mezzanine tranches (CoreWeave's $3.1B DDTL 5.0, SOFR+450, BA2/BB+) trade like junk — same borrower, wildly different pricing. Whose payment promise sits above the GPU collateral is the real question, not the GPU price itself.
Chewable specifics: CoreWeave 5-yr CDS round-tripped from ~881bps (Dec 2025) to ~452bps (early June) back to ~855bps (late July) — implying ~50% five-year default probability by market pricing, a second visit to the same stress peak, not a new regime. Its $2.6B delayed-draw term loan (Anthropic/Jane Street/Hudson River Trading deployments) opened mid-July at SOFR+425-450 at 99 cents, widened to SOFR+500 at 97 cents by July 29 — ~100-125bps plus 2 points of OID repriced in under three weeks. Balance sheet: $25.1B principal debt ($7.5B current) vs. $2.2B cash at Q1; $536M quarterly net interest expense; operating cash flow $3.0B ran ~$4.7B short of $7.7B equipment purchases; $6.1B principal due through end-2026, $5.7B more in 2027. The "wrong-way risk" thought experiment: a customer locked at $2.50/GPU-hour while the forward market falls to $1.60 is 56% above market — the incentive to renegotiate/dispute rises exactly as the lender's collateral value falls. Comparables: Nebius raised $775M at SOFR+250 against an investment-grade customer; IREN got $3.65B at a 6% blended cost backing a Microsoft contract; AMD reportedly had to backstop a Crusoe GPU rental to support a ~$300M loan at 6% — what lenders won't take without a guarantee.
BACKUP (unused, held in reserve): ctvc-ldes-hyperscaler-buyer — CTVC/Currence, "Long Duration Energy Storage Finds Its Buyer" (Aug 10): data centers have overtaken government procurement as the key LDES demand driver, with "bring-your-own-capacity" deals letting a data center jump a utility interconnection queue by pairing with firm storage capacity — Form Energy's 300MW iron-air deal with Xcel/Google at Pine Island is the first commercial-scale proof point.

Three years of genetic monitoring ahead to confirm the population stabilizes. (Sources: Euronews, Australian Geographic, Odonata Foundation.)
