## SEGMENT THREE — DEEP DIVE

SAM: For the deep dive, I want to stay at the Supreme Court, but a different kind of procedural accident from the one we covered Tuesday. The case is BMG Rights Management versus Vetter, docket 25-1391, and on Monday the Court disposed of it without deciding anything, because it could not form a quorum. Our sources are Music Business Worldwide, Complete Music Update, Bloomberg Law and IPWatchdog.
ALEX: Start with the song, because it matters. "Double Shot, Of My Baby's Love", the 1960s hit, was written by a Louisiana songwriter and publisher named Cyril Vetter. Under section 304 of the Copyright Act, authors can terminate old grants of their copyright and take the rights back. Vetter did that, and the dispute is about how far the reclaimed rights reach.
SAM: The Fifth Circuit, in Vetter versus Resnik, held that termination recaptures worldwide rights, not only US rights. The logic is about the phrase "arise under this title". The court read it as pointing to the source of the rights, which is US law, rather than the place where the song is exploited. And the original grant had purported to transfer the song throughout the world, so the termination reverts it throughout the world.
ALEX: The music publishers hit the panic button. The petitioners are BMG, Capitol Christian Music Group from Universal, Essential Music Publishing from Sony and Warner-Tamerlane, with Paul Clement arguing for them. Their argument is that a worldwide reversion upends global licensing and catalog valuations, since the majors have bought and financed these catalogs on the assumption that foreign rights would not snap back.
SAM: Then the Court's order on Monday: Justices Sotomayor, Gorsuch, Barrett and Jackson took no part. That leaves 5 justices, and a quorum of the Court is 6. When there is no quorum and a majority of the qualified justices cannot hear a case, 28 U.S.C. 2109 directs that the lower court judgment be affirmed, with the same effect as upon affirmance by an equally divided court.
ALEX: Here is the distinction from Tuesday's explainer. A 4-4 tie happens when 8 justices heard it and split. This is a no-quorum affirmance, where too few justices are eligible to sit at all. The effect is the same, no national precedent, and the Fifth Circuit's rule binds only federal courts in Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas. The Court used the same mechanism in Baker versus Coates in 2024.
SAM: Why were 4 justices out? Reporting by Music Business Worldwide, CMU and Bloomberg Law frames it as a publishing-ownership hook: Bertelsmann owns BMG and also Penguin Random House, which has published books by all 4 justices. I'd stress that is the outlets' framing of the reason, since the justices did not explain themselves in the order.
ALEX: And that is the contested ground. First, is the Fifth Circuit right on the text? A textualist can argue foreign rights arise under foreign law, in which case a US termination should not reach them. Second, forum shopping: songwriters now have a rational reason to file in the Fifth Circuit. Third, the Vetter side argued this was an unsuitable vehicle, since the publishers did not challenge a separate ownership holding.
SAM: The ethics question is the one I keep returning to. Fix the Court has long argued for more recusals, and the Court has no enforceable ethics code. If book income from a conglomerate is enough to sit out, then every case involving Penguin Random House's parent could hit the same wall. If it is not enough, then recusal looks arbitrary. Either way, the Court has now let a procedural accident settle a question worth hundreds of millions of dollars.
ALEX: For this audience, the second-order read is bargaining power. Termination windows are opening for 1960s through 1980s catalogs. A worldwide reversion right strengthens songwriters in every renegotiation, because the label's alternative to a deal gets worse. A related cert petition exists, according to Patently-O, so a cleaner vehicle may come back. Until then, the Fifth Circuit is the most songwriter-friendly venue in American copyright law.

## SEGMENT SEVEN — MANDARIN VOCAB

SAM: Two Mandarin words today, both from the CATL story you'll hear in a moment. The first is a connective, 反倒, fǎndào, meaning "on the contrary" or "instead", for a result that runs against expectation.
ALEX: It's the conversational cousin of 反而. Both mark a surprise, but 反而 is neutral and shows up in writing as easily as speech, while 反倒 is a little more spoken and a little more emphatic. It typically follows a clause that sets up an expectation. 收入大涨，利润反倒增长有限。(Revenue surged, but profit instead grew only a little.)
SAM: Notice the structure: the first clause promises a good outcome, and 反倒 delivers the letdown. You can also use it for a bad situation producing a good result. 价格下跌了，销量反倒上升了。(Prices fell, but sales instead rose.)
ALEX: The second word is 利润, lìrùn, "profit", a finance-desk term from HSK 4. It is neutral to formal and collocates with 空间, as in 利润空间, "profit margin room", with 率, as in 利润率, and with verbs like 压缩, to squeeze, or 侵蚀, to erode. 原材料成本上升，压缩了企业的利润空间。(Rising raw-material costs squeezed companies' profit margins.)
SAM: And how does it differ from 收益 and 盈利? 收益 is returns, usually from an investment, 盈利 is earnings and often works like a verb, as in "the company is profitable", while 利润 is the accounting profit line on the income statement. Next up, the story that uses both.

## SEGMENT TEN — OP-EDS & COMMENTARY

SAM: Today's argument comes from the bench, not a columnist. On Tuesday, Justice Elena Kagan spoke at Catholic University's law school, in conversation with the law professor J. ALEX: The second counter comes from her own side. UCLA's Rick Hasen said she didn't go far enough, calling Callais the worst election-law decision in 100 years at the Supreme Court. A justice who is stuck in dissent has run out of formal tools, and speeches are what's left.
SAM: My own read is that both critiques can be true. The remarks matter less as a legal argument, because she has already made that in the dissents, than as a signal that the Court's own members see the legitimacy question as live.
