## VOCAB OF THE DAY (candidates, tied to today's stories, checked fresh vs. the 58-word ledger)
- Mandarin 1: 分歧 (fēnqí, noun) — "difference of opinion / divergence" — ties to the Fed's expected hawkish dissents (Logan, Hammack) and the diverging oil/AI-capex forces. Register: 书面语/formal, standard in financial commentary. Near-synonym contrast: 分裂 (fēnliè) is stronger — an actual rupture/split — while 分歧 is a difference of view within a still-functioning body.
- Mandarin 2: 未必 (wèibì, adverb/connective) — "not necessarily" — ties to Visa's clean beat that the market punished anyway ("达标未必意味着上涨" — hitting the number doesn't necessarily mean a rally). Near-synonym contrast: 不一定 (bù yīdìng) is the everyday spoken equivalent; 未必 is more formal/written, common in financial argument and understatement.
- Tagalog 1: gumaan (verb, "became lighter/eased") — ties to the peso's pressure easing over three sessions before the overnight oil spike. Natural, common register.
- Tagalog 2: pag-iingat (noun, "carefulness/caution") — ties to both PAGASA's flood advisory language and BSP's cautious, watchful policy stance today.
