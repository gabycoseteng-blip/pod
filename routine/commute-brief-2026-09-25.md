## 10. MANDARIN VOCAB (2 words, tied to China segment) — scaffolded (English explain, 汉字
    examples w/ immediate translation)
- 搁置 (gēzhì) — to shelve, set aside, table (an issue) indefinitely; abstract, formal/书面语.
  Contrast w/ 拖延 (tuōyán, "to delay/procrastinate" — implies avoidance/inefficiency, more
  personal/negative) vs 搁置 (deliberate, institutional, often indefinite). Ties to Taiwan/AI
  being explicitly carved out of the Trump-Xi truce.
- 除非…否则 (chúfēi…fǒuzé) — "unless… (otherwise)" — advanced HSK-4 connective. Contrast w/
  只要…就 (zhǐyào…jiù, "as long as… then" — positive/enabling conditional) vs 除非…否则
  (negative/exclusionary conditional — names the one condition that would change things).
  Ties to: tensions stay shelved unless Taiwan/AI issues get addressed.
