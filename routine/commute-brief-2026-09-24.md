## SEGMENT 7 — MANDARIN VOCAB (taught here, used in China segment next)
- **缓解 (huǎnjiě)** — to ease/alleviate (pressure, tension, a crisis) WITHOUT fully resolving it. Formal/written register (书面语), common in economic and diplomatic news. Near-synonym contrast: 解决 (jiějué, "to resolve/solve completely") — 缓解 only lessens severity, doesn't fix the underlying problem. Collocations: 缓解压力/缓解紧张局势/缓解危机/缓解负担.
- **让步 (ràngbù)** — to make a concession, to yield/back down (usually one side, often under pressure). Formal/news register. Near-synonym contrast: 妥协 (tuǒxié, "compromise" — implies BOTH sides give ground toward a middle point) vs 让步 (specifically one side yielding). Collocations: 做出让步/作出让步/被迫让步/拒绝让步.
