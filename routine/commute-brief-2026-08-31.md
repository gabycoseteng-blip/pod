## DEEP DIVE
**PICK: Adam Tooze, Chartbook #472 — "Neo-Burgundian technocracy: Ernst Jünger's reckoning
with nihilism" (Aug 29, 2026).** Source path: Gmail inbox newsletter.
Tooze reads Jünger's 1939 novella *On the Marble Cliffs* — written while Jünger (WWI hero
turned Weimar-era nihilist-technocrat prophet) deliberately kept distance from the Third
Reich — as a self-reckoning with his own earlier cult of violent, technological power. Setting
fuses fast cars with medieval aristocratic livery ("New Burgundy"); Tooze argues the key
character Braquemart is Jünger's psychological self-portrait of the "nihilist captain of
power," and explicitly rejects reading the villain (the Oberförster) as simply Hitler or
Stalin — the real subject is a *type*, the technocrat who treats ideas as weapons and life as
clockwork.
Why it matters to this listener: the "high-tech power fused with dynastic/feudal hierarchy and
contempt for the masses" archetype is exactly the cultural lens now applied to today's AI/
data-center oligarchy discourse (tech barons as neo-feudal lords, private power/land/water
empires) — rhymes with the buildout story without recapping it.
Discussion material:
- Jünger held Germany's highest WWI valor decoration (Pour le Mérite), wrote *Storm of Steel*
  (1920); by the 1930s a "nationalist prophet of technological modernity" who then, unlike
  Heidegger and Carl Schmitt, kept the regime at arm's length.
- Novella's world merges eras: "powerful cars with powerful lights" driven by men in medieval
  feudal livery — Tooze's "feudal-sci-fi mix," explicitly compared to Roy Batty's "tears in
  rain" monologue in *Blade Runner*.
- Braquemart: "a cold, rootless intelligence with a penchant for utopias" who "insisted that
  ideas be armed, tooth and claw" — Jünger's portrait of the man he once was.
- Tooze rejects the Hitler/Stalin allegory as "simplistic and implausible" — villain =
  unleashed wild violence, not a specific dictator's project.
- Quotable: "there is a profound difference between fully formed nihilism and unchecked
  anarchy" — one wants slaves and captive armies, the other wants to return civilization to
  wild beasts.
- Jünger's eventual answer to nihilism, per Tooze: "the best weapon against nihilism is the
  change of the human heart."
Backup (if collision): "Road To Carry" newsletter, "Pool Services: The Next Home Services
Frontier?" (Aug 29) — PE roll-up thesis in the $9B pool-cleaning industry (Pool Troopers,
25 acquisitions in 44 months, folded into SPS PoolCare Jan 2026); multiple arbitrage (2.5-5x
SDE owner-operator → 7-10x EBITDA platform); parallels fragmented infra-service-market
