## 6. DEEP DIVE — source: SemiAnalysis newsletter (inbox)
"$12B of US ratepayers' money wasted on a modeling mistake and PJM wants to do it again" — SemiAnalysis (energy team w/ Nathan Iyer), published Aug 16, 2026.
- 6-month reverse-engineering of PJM's black-box "Reserve Requirement Study" (sets how much capacity PJM buys at auction).
- Finds PJM undercredits its own existing fleet by ~3.1-4.7GW because it (1) never credits cold-air turbine uprating (up to 25% more output in winter; PJM's own class table shows 8.4% avg, credited at zero) and (2) ignores post-Storm-Elliott winterization — 400 of ~750 thermal units filed winterization upgrades by Jan 2024, yet PJM still weights 2022 Elliott failure data at full force.
- PJM's supply curve is nearly flat (79% of bids at $2-5/MW-day) then spikes vertically near the requirement ($270-352/MW-day) — so a small correction swings the whole auction. Correcting the model would have cleared the 2025/26 auction $6.7B cheaper (needing just 14MW less procurement) and the 2026/27 auction $4.9B cheaper — $11.6B combined (sensitivity range $8.0-14.5B), out of $63.6B total paid across four auctions.
- Read-through to the Sept 30-Oct 21 emergency/backstop auction (results Dec 2): PJM's model says a 6.8GW shortfall; SemiAnalysis's corrected model says ~3.0GW.
- PJM's Board (Jul 27) overrode a stakeholder-endorsed voluntary-subscription design (2/3 sector support) for a $555/MW-day price-cap, pay-as-bid structure with no committed counterparty. Contracts run to 2043; participating load-serving entities post $1.5M/MW credit support; max liability on 6.8GW ≈ $21B.
- A "Package C" winterization-accreditation fix passed PJM's own task force 178-54 (77%) in July 2025, then was killed by a senior committee 26 days later with only 30.7% support.
- FERC comment deadline on PJM's backstop filing: Aug 21, 2026 — three days after air date.
- Facts to cite: existing generators' own bids ($8-14/MW-day per PJM's Independent Market Monitor) vs. clearing prices 20-40x higher; median existing combined-cycle plant made 407% of going-forward costs from energy/ancillary markets alone in 2025, before any capacity payment.
