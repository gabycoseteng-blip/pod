## DEEP DIVE — source: inbox (The Economist daily newsletter, Sept 21 2026)
**"Daddy's Girl"** — Review of Economic Studies paper (1993-2017 Danish
registry data, owner-managed single-manager firms) uses the birth of a manager's
first child as a natural experiment: when a male boss's FIRST child is a
daughter rather than a son, women's relative earnings at his firm rise ~4.4% and
female employment share rises ~2.9%, essentially immediately. Answers a normally
unanswerable question — is managerial gender bias a fixed trait or does lived
experience shift it? Real, measurable effect on pay/hiring from one random
biological event. Ties into live corporate-governance/DEI debates: is fair pay a
matter of incentives and rules, or of who's deciding and what they've lived
through? Attribution: The Economist newsletter (editor Stephanie Studer, Sept 21
issue) covering the Review of Economic Studies paper; also covered by Aarhus BSS,
Marginal Revolution, CBS News. Genuine change of domain (labor econ) after a run
of tech-capex/energy/macro throughlines.
