## SEGMENT SEVEN — MANDARIN VOCAB
SAM: Mandarin vocab, tied to our China story. First phrase: 只有…才…, zhǐyǒu... cái.
ALEX: 只有…才…, only if, then. It states a strict necessary condition — without this one thing, nothing else gets you there.
SAM: "只有房地产市场真正企稳，央行才有可能进一步放松货币政策。" Only if the property market genuinely stabilizes will the central bank be able to further loosen monetary policy.
ALEX: Formal register, 书面语 — and worth contrasting with 一旦…就…, once, then, which we taught before. 一旦…就… marks an immediate trigger; 只有…才… says the opposite logical shape — this is the one precondition, nothing else substitutes.
SAM: Second word: 背离, bèilí.
ALEX: 背离 — to deviate from, to diverge from. An abstract verb, also formal register, that collocates with 趋势, a trend, or 走势, a market movement.
SAM: "债市持续走强，股市却屡创新高，两者的走势已经明显背离。" Bonds keep strengthening while equities keep hitting new highs — the two have clearly diverged.
ALEX: It's a near-synonym of 分化, to split into subgroups, which is more neutral. 背离 specifically flags two things that used to move together now visibly pulling apart.
